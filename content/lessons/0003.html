Vamos conversar como se eu estivesse aí do seu lado 🙂

O Simple Present (Presente Simples) é o tempo verbal que você usa quando quer falar de coisas que acontecem sempre, quase sempre ou que são verdades (fatos). Pense assim: é o tempo das rotinas e dos hábitos.

💡 Dica rápida: se você consegue colocar “todo dia”, “sempre”, “geralmente”, “às vezes” na frase, o Simple Present provavelmente é a melhor escolha.

📘 Explicação

✅ Forma afirmativa

  • I / You / We / They + verbo (forma base): I play, They study.
  • He / She / It + verbo com -s (ou -es): She plays, He watches.

🧠 Atenção especial no He/She/It: aqui é onde muita gente erra. Se for ele/ela/isso, normalmente o verbo ganha um -s.

📌 Regras rápidas do -s / -es

  • Geral: adiciona -s → play → plays
  • Termina em -ch, -sh, -x, -s, -o: adiciona -es → watch → watches
  • Termina em consoante + y: troca y por ies → study → studies

❌ Forma negativa

  • I / You / We / They → don’t + verbo base: I don’t like.
  • He / She / It → doesn’t + verbo base: She doesn’t like.

👉 Repara comigo: quando entra doesn’t, o verbo volta para a forma base (sem -s). Por isso é She doesn’t study (não “studies”).

❓ Perguntas

  • Do + sujeito + verbo base? (I/you/we/they) → Do you play?
  • Does + sujeito + verbo base? (he/she/it) → Does he play?

⏰ Palavras comuns com Simple Present: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week, on Mondays, at night.

🗣️ Exemplos

  • Rotina: I wake up early on weekdays.
  • Rotina: We go to school in the morning.
  • Hábito: They play video games after class.
  • Fato: Cats like milk.
  • Fato: The sun sets in the west.
  • She studies English every day.
  • He works in a hospital.
  • My brother watches TV at night.
  • My mom goes to the gym on Mondays.
  • It rains a lot in January.
  • I don’t like coffee.
  • They don’t speak French.
  • She doesn’t eat meat.
  • He doesn’t play basketball.
  • It doesn’t work very well.
  • Do you like pizza?
  • Do they live near here?
  • Does she study at night?
  • Does he watch cartoons?
  • Does your teacher give homework every day?
  • Always: I always brush my teeth before bed.
  • Usually: She usually walks to school.
  • Often: We often listen to music.
  • Sometimes: They sometimes eat out.
  • Never: He never drinks soda.

🎧 Pronúncia

🎯 Treino rápido: leia 3 frases em voz alta e preste atenção no som do -s no final (como em plays, studies, watches).
